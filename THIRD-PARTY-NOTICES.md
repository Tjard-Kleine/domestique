## Coucou

Parts of the animation code are ported from [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) by Louis Raillé:
the open spring and close curve of the island (`src/Domestique.App/Springs.cs`, `src/Domestique.App/Island.cs`),
